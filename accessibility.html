Website Accessibility

Ensuring our construction & property solutions website is accessible to everyone.

At Sought Consult, we believe everyone should be able to access and use our website with ease.

We aim to provide a website experience that is clear, accessible and convenient for all visitors, including people with disabilities.

Our Accessibility Objectives Include:

  • Clear and readable text: Using clean typographic hierarchy, generous line heights, and scalable font sizing.
  • Sufficient colour contrast: Applying strict WCAG AA contrast standards across our Black, White, and Gold color palette.
  • Descriptive form labels: Ensuring all form fields have explicit, legible label elements.
  • Simple and consistent navigation: Providing intuitive, standard navigation structures across all pages.
  • Keyboard accessibility wherever possible: Supporting keyboard navigation across tabs, dropdowns, and interactive buttons.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images: Providing descriptive alt text for all photography, blueprints, and diagrams.
  • Clearly identifiable buttons and links: Distinct hover, focus, and active styling on all interactive elements.
  • Responsive layouts across mobile, tablet and desktop devices: Fully adaptive layouts that do not clip, overflow, or hinder mobile readability.
